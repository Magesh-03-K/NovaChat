Data storage & retention

NovaChat implements a zero-retention policy for plaintext content. All message content is mathematically indecipherable to our engineering teams and hosting providers.

Undelivered queued messages are retained for a maximum of 14 calendar days in transient memory queues, after which they are permanently culled if recipients remain offline.
